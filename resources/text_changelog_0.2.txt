# Squire v0.2

## What's new

This release makes it easier to get back into a conversation and follow it on a small screen.

## Conversations

- Up opens your last conversation with recent messages, and Back from a quick-launched conversation returns home.
- Ask Again in the Down menu lets you reuse a recent question.
- Long answers stay open while the agent is working.

## Watch

- What's New is now in the Down menu.
- Touch controls!
