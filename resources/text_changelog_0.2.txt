# Squire v0.2

## What's new

This release makes Telegram sign-in reliable and easier to recover from.

### Signing in

- Your phone number is remembered once you confirm it, so retrying sign-in does not mean typing it again.
- The "Connecting" and "Verifying" screens now clear themselves when sign-in finishes, fails, or is interrupted by a notification.

### Getting around

- Back from a quick-launched conversation now returns to the Squire home screen instead of closing the app.
- Quick launch still starts listening immediately; after the inactivity timeout it returns home.
- Up now shows your recent prompts when Telegram history is available, so you can repeat one without retyping it. The canned prompts remain the fallback.
- The Down menu now has Conversation, which reopens your last chat with the last few messages.
- Starting a question now continues the last thread and shows its last two messages for context.
- A long answer no longer closes the conversation at the quick-launch timeout; it stays open while the agent is still working.

### Under the hood

- Telegram now uses the maintained teleproto client instead of the deprecated GramJS.
- Fixed WebSocket connections through the Pebble phone app.
